## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: About Page Content

The about page SHALL display accurate company information from the official business plan including the actual founding team, mission statement, and company description.

#### Scenario: Display founding team

- **WHEN** a user visits the about page
- **THEN** the team section displays the five founding executives:
  - Temitayo Sunmonu-Balogun (CEO)
  - Emmanuel Orevba (COO)
  - Eric Ojeaga (CFO)
  - Kevin Odiley (CTO)
  - Kikelomo Abikele (Head, Investment & Partnerships)

#### Scenario: Display mission statement

- **WHEN** a user views the mission section
- **THEN** the content reflects the business plan mission of channeling diaspora remittances into pre-vetted investment opportunities

### Requirement: Homepage Content

The homepage SHALL accurately represent NaijaConnect Capital's products, services, and value proposition as defined in the business plan.

#### Scenario: Display company tagline

- **WHEN** a user visits the homepage
- **THEN** the hero section describes the platform as transforming diaspora remittances into productive capital investments

#### Scenario: Display projected statistics

- **WHEN** a user views the stats section
- **THEN** statistics align with business plan projections (10,000 investors target, ₦3-5B investment goal)

#### Scenario: Display product offerings

- **WHEN** a user views the features/products section
- **THEN** the four core products are highlighted: RemitConnect, InvestDirect, ImpactTrack Dashboard, Diaspora Learning Hub

### Requirement: Contact Page Information

The contact page SHALL display accurate contact information from the business plan.

#### Scenario: Display contact details

- **WHEN** a user visits the contact page
- **THEN** the following information is displayed:
  - Email: info@naijaconnect.com.ng
  - Office: Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria
  - Hours: Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM WAT

### Requirement: Footer Content

The footer SHALL display current copyright information and accurate company description.

#### Scenario: Display copyright

- **WHEN** a user views the footer on any page
- **THEN** copyright shows 2025 and accurate company tagline
