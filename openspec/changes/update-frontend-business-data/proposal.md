# Update Frontend with NaijaConnect Capital Business Data

**Change ID:** `update-frontend-business-data`  
**Status:** Draft  
**Created:** 2025-12-13  
**Author:** Development Team

## Executive Summary

Update the frontend application to reflect the actual NaijaConnect Capital Company Limited business plan data, replacing placeholder content with accurate company information, team profiles, product descriptions, and market positioning.

## Problem Statement

The current frontend contains generic placeholder content that does not accurately represent NaijaConnect Capital's:

- Actual founding team members and their roles
- True company mission, vision, and values
- Real product/service offerings (RemitConnect, InvestDirect, ImpactTrack Dashboard)
- Accurate market positioning and value propositions
- Official company registration details and regulatory framework

This misalignment between the frontend and the official business plan creates:

- Inconsistent brand messaging
- Potential legal/compliance issues with inaccurate representations
- Poor user trust due to generic content
- Missed opportunities to communicate the platform's unique value proposition

## Proposed Solution

Replace all placeholder content across the frontend with data from the official NaijaConnect Capital business plan, including:

1. **Company Information**

   - Official business name: NaijaConnect Capital Company Limited
   - Business address: Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria
   - Contact details and official communication channels
   - Registration details (pending CAMA 2020 registration)

2. **Team Profiles**

   - Temitayo Sunmonu-Balogun (CEO)
   - Emmanuel Orevba (COO)
   - Eric Ojeaga (CFO)
   - Kevin Odiley (CTO)
   - Kikelomo Abikele (Head, Investment & Partnerships)

3. **Product/Service Descriptions**

   - RemitConnect: Low-cost remittance gateway (2.0% fee)
   - InvestDirect: Digital marketplace (2% annual management fee)
   - ImpactTrack Dashboard: Free investment tracking tool
   - Diaspora Learning Hub: Educational portal (₦5,000/course)

4. **Market Positioning**

   - Target market: 17 million Nigerians abroad
   - Value proposition: Transform remittances into productive investments
   - Competitive advantages: Transparency, regulatory compliance, impact tracking

5. **Features & Benefits**
   - Update feature descriptions to match actual platform capabilities
   - Align pricing information with the business plan
   - Incorporate ESG and social impact messaging
